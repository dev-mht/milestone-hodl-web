MHT Liquidity Pioneer Program

Support Milestone Hodl Token (MHT) liquidity, help secure the protocol, and earn ecosystem rewards. Slots are strictly limited and fully funded by the marketing wallet.

Introduction

Purpose

The Milestone Hodl Token (MHT) project reached a historic milestone toward full decentralization on May 5, 2026, with the official renouncement of Smart Contract ownership — the on-chain code is now immutable and fully autonomous.

To support this transition and strengthen order book depth, the team is launching the Liquidity Pioneer pilot program. Fully funded by a fixed, capped allocation from the marketing wallet, this program rewards community members who add and lock healthy liquidity on the MHT/WBNB pair (PancakeSwap).
85 Slots · 3 Tiers

Mechanism

How to Participate

Four steps. Reserving your slot first protects you from locking liquidity for a slot that may already be full.

01
Reserve Your Slot
Go to our Reservation page and tell us which tier and lock duration you want. You'll get an immediate confirmation on whether a slot is currently available — do this before adding any liquidity.
02
Buy & Pair
Once your slot is confirmed, buy the amount of MHT needed to match the BNB value of your tier (0.1 / 0.5 / 1 BNB).
03
Add Liquidity
Deposit your BNB and MHT into the PancakeSwap pool to receive your MHT/WBNB LP tokens.
04
Lock & Submit
Lock your LP tokens on PinkLock (PinkSale) for 30 or 90 days, then submit your wallet address and Tx Hash through the confirmation link from step 1. Once verified, your distribution schedule activates.

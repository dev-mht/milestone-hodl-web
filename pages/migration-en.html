01 — How it works
  • 1 V2 = 1 V3 + bonus. The bonus depends on when you migrate (schedule below).
  • One atomic transaction. migrateAll() sends your V2 to the burn address and delivers your V3 in the same call. Either both happen, or nothing does.
  • Capped by the snapshot. Each address can migrate up to the V2 it held in the snapshot — no more, even if it buys V2 after the snapshot.
  • No admin. The migration contract has no owner, no withdrawal function, no editable date or cap.
  • Nothing is taken back. V3 not claimed by the end of the window is burned by a public function — never returned to the team.
  • Selling right after launch? The V3 pool starts with MHT only: BNB enters the pool when people buy. Until there are buyers, a sale only meets a small 0.05 BNB safety band locked under the launch price. Even if every migrated token were sold at once, sellers would share 0.05 BNB in total and the price would crash. Liquidity grows with every buy.

04 — Manual method (BscScan)

Prefer not to connect your wallet to a website? Do it directly on BscScan, with the official addresses below (they appear here as soon as the contracts are live).

  1. Open the migration contract: published at launch → Read Contract → migratableOf with your address. Copy the number exactly (it is in wei, 18 decimals).
  2. Open the V2 token: 0x22E0fcEc929c4F38c8D8c03B2B2F225E98F133fa → Write Contract → Connect to Web3 → approve: spender = the migration contract, amount = the number you copied.
  3. Back on the migration contract → Write Contract → migrateAll → Write. Your V3 arrives in the same transaction.
  4. Add the V3 token to your wallet with its address: published at launch.
05 — Stay safe
  • The only official page is milestonehodl.apexpad.io. Check the address bar.
  • You approve the exact amount you migrate — never an unlimited amount.
  • The team will never DM you, ask for your seed phrase, or ask you to send tokens anywhere. Migration never requires sending V2 to a person.
  • Both contracts are verified on BscScan. Compare the addresses on this page with the ones announced on X and Telegram.
06 — The snapshot

Final snapshot, taken on 14 September 2026 shortly before deployment: 18 addresses, 14,605,045 MHT in total. These caps are written into the migration contract. Amounts are truncated to whole tokens.

Not in the snapshot: the V2 contract itself (its balance is frozen forever, see the vault status report), the PancakeSwap V2 pool, the burn address, the project's marketing and founder wallets (the founder's V3 allocation is separate and locked for one year in a custodian), a 0x fee-collector address, and dust balances under 1 MHT.
